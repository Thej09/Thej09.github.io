Delete your Swoop account

How to permanently delete your Swoop account and data.

In the app (quickest)

  1. Open Swoop and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete account & progress.
  3. Tap Delete everything to confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away.

If you can't use the app

Email thejanelankayer@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Swoop account". Swoop accounts are anonymous, so to help us find yours, include any Online display names you used and roughly when you last played. We'll delete the matching account within 30 days and let you know.

If you've already uninstalled Swoop, the progress saved on your phone was removed along with the app.

What gets deleted

  • Your anonymous Swoop account
  • Daily Challenge attempts and results
  • Online display names, game results, and blocks
  • Reports you made, and reports made about you
  • When you delete in the app: everything saved on your phone, including Adventure progress, coins, lives, shop items and settings

What's kept

Nothing linked to you is kept after deletion. Online rooms you were part of may carry on for the other players, without your name or results.
