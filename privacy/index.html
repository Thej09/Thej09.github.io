Privacy policy

Swoop · Effective 6 November 2026

Swoop is made by Thejan Elankayer. This policy explains what information the Swoop app ("Swoop", "we", "us") collects, why, and the choices you have. Swoop doesn't ask for your name, email address, phone number or contacts, and you don't need to sign up to play.

Information stored on your phone

Your progress is saved on your device: Adventure levels, coins, lives, power-ups, shop items, customisations and settings. It stays on your phone. The only gameplay information that leaves it is the optional usage stats described below.

Your anonymous account

When you first use a feature that needs our servers, such as Daily Challenge rankings or Online play, Swoop creates an anonymous account. It's a random identifier with no name or email attached. We use it to run those features and to keep results fair.

  • Daily Challenge: which puzzle you played, the difficulty, when you started and how long you took. These are used to work out your ranking, such as "Top 9%". Other players only see overall rankings, never your individual times.
  • Online play: the display name you choose, room names, and your moves, progress and finishing times in each game. Your display name, progress and results are shown to the other players in the same room. Names are checked against a filter for offensive words.
  • Reports and blocks: if you report or block a player, we store who reported or blocked whom, the room, the reason and the reported name, so we can review reports and keep blocked players apart.

This information is stored by our hosting provider, Supabase, which processes it on our behalf. Like most online services, their servers may also log technical details such as your IP address to deliver and secure the service.

Ads

Swoop is free and is supported by optional rewarded ads: you choose to watch one in exchange for a reward. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier, approximate location based on your IP address, device and app details, and how you interact with ads.

  • Consent: in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, you're asked for consent before ads are personalised. You can still play and watch ads if you decline. Those ads just aren't personalised.
  • iPhone and iPad: Swoop only uses your device's advertising identifier if you allow tracking when asked. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings, under Privacy & Security, then Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.
  • Changing your choices: where the law gives you a choice, including the right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information under some US state laws, an ad privacy option appears in Swoop's Settings.

Learn more about how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Usage stats

To understand how Swoop is played and make it better, Swoop sends anonymous usage stats to PostHog, our analytics provider, which processes them on our behalf. These include things like app opens and session length, levels started, finished or failed, power-ups used, coins spent, and rewarded ads offered and watched, along with the app version and whether you're on iPhone or Android. They're linked to a random identifier, and to your anonymous account if you have one, never to your name or email, and include no free text. Like most online services, PostHog may use your IP address to estimate a rough location, such as your country.

You can turn this off at any time in Swoop's Settings with Share usage stats. When it's off, nothing more is sent.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your information for money.
  • We don't use session recordings, screen captures or crash-reporting tools, and we don't use usage stats for advertising.
  • We don't sell coins or anything else for real money.

How long we keep information

We keep your anonymous account and the information linked to it until you delete it. Deleting your account permanently erases your account, Daily results, Online names and results, blocks, and reports made by or about you. Rooms you were in carry on without you.

Usage stats are kept for up to 12 months and then deleted. Deleting your account in the app doesn't remove usage stats already sent, so if you'd like those deleted too, email us and we'll erase them.

Deleting your information

In the app, open Settings and tap Delete account & progress. This erases your account on our servers and everything saved on your phone. It can't be undone. If you can't use the app, see how to ask us to delete your account.

Children

Swoop isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has used Online play, contact us and we'll delete the account.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to object to how it's used. Because Swoop accounts are anonymous, the quickest way to delete your information is from inside the app. For anything else, contact us.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update it here and change the effective date above.
